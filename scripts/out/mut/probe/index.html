    1Fee cycle

    Swaps generate fees. SAME routes those fees into purchases, delivering each bought token to an unspendable address. Demand rises slightly, supply drops slightly, so newcomers meet leaner conditions.

    Activity powers the engine; the engine rewards activity. Busy periods speed things up, calm periods slow them without waste.

    2Why loops stall

    Many designs borrow this concept, then stumble. Four failure shapes recur.

    bursty
    Proceeds idle for long periods, then arrive together and jolt charts.
    manual
    Somebody must press a button. Eventually nobody does.
    mixed funds
    Spending taps wallets holding unrelated money, muddying accounting.
    bad timing
    Orders hit thin books, costing extra while lasting briefly.

    All four reduce to timing or custody. SAME removes both variables.

    3Pace follows participation

    Order size scales with market activity. Heavy trading brings larger purchases; light trading brings smaller ones while leftovers queue. Ceiling stays a fraction of recent turnover, keeping SAME smaller than its crowd.

    4Cycle anatomy

    Every pass performs five moves, fixed order, zero discretion.

    moveeffect
    gatherCollect fees produced by swaps; only newly arrived funds join pool.
    reserveHold back modest buffer for network costs.
    measureCompute order size, bounded by recent turnover.
    purchaseAcquire token through public liquidity.
    retireForward precisely what was purchased toward a dead end; supply travels one direction.

    Books stay narrow deliberately: pool holds arrivals; retirement touches purchases only.

    5Boundaries

    No selling path exists. Reserved purchase funds stay put, and orders never exceed absorption capacity. Every action leaves a Solana trace, so after the contract address appears anybody can compare this page against chain.
